# Answer review: Wordle, Crossword and Word Search

Every answer word of the three games, by language and category, with the meaning and the description(s) the game shows today.
Source files: `Assets/Main/Data/Wordle/Levels`, `Crossword/Puzzles`, `Word Search/Puzzles` (all three languages).

**Status column**

- **WRONG** - the answer word does not mean what the game says, or it is not the everyday word for it.
- **MEANING** - the word is real, but the English meaning the game gives is off or too narrow (for example sanggol, 'baby': it is an infant; amiga: a female friend).
- **CHECK** - the word may be right but is rare, a different dialect form or a borrowed word; a native speaker should decide.
- **CLUE** - the word is fine but the description is bad (grammar jargon, several unrelated meanings, too long).
- (empty) - looks fine.

I am not a native speaker of any of the three languages. WRONG and CHECK are my judgement and, for many of them, the source dictionary file itself
lists the odd meaning, so please have a native speaker confirm each one before the data is changed.

Totals: 407 unique answers, **25 WRONG**, **29 MEANING**, **36 CHECK**, **8 CLUE**.
